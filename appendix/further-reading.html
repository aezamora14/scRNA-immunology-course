Further Reading

The resources below are stable, maintained and free. Verify links; software documentation moves.

Comprehensive references

  • Single-cell best practices (Heumos, Schaar, Lance et al., Nature Reviews Genetics, 2023; online book at https://www.sc-best-practices.org). The most complete current treatment, Python-centred but conceptually language-neutral. Chapters on quality control, normalization, integration, differential expression and clonotype analysis align directly with this course.
  • Orchestrating Single-Cell Analysis with Bioconductor (https://bioconductor.org/books/OSCA/). The R/Bioconductor equivalent; excellent on the statistics of QC, normalization and feature selection.
  • Luecken & Theis, “Current best practices in single-cell RNA-seq analysis: a tutorial”, Molecular Systems Biology, 2019. Shorter and slightly dated but still the best single-paper overview.

  • scirpy: https://scirpy.scverse.org

  • CellTypist: https://www.celltypist.org
  • Harmony: https://portals.broadinstitute.org/harmony/
  • scvi-tools: https://scvi-tools.org

Key methods papers (by week)

Week 1. Lun et al., “EmptyDrops”, Genome Biology, 2019 (cell calling). Germain et al., “scDblFinder”, F1000Research, 2021. Wolock et al., “Scrublet”, Cell Systems, 2019. Young & Behjati, “SoupX”, GigaScience, 2020 (ambient RNA).

Week 2. Hafemeister & Satija, “Normalization and variance stabilization of single-cell RNA-seq data using regularized negative binomial regression”, Genome Biology, 2019 (SCTransform). Ahlmann-Eltze & Huber, “Comparison of transformations for single-cell RNA-seq data”, Nature Methods, 2023.

Week 3. Traag, Waltman & van Eck, “From Louvain to Leiden”, Scientific Reports, 2019. McInnes, Healy & Melville, “UMAP”, arXiv 1802.03426, 2018. Chari & Pachter, “The specious art of single-cell genomics”, PLOS Computational Biology, 2023 (a critique of over-interpreting embeddings that every student should read). Zappia & Oshlack, “Clustering trees”, GigaScience, 2018.

Week 4. Hao et al., “Integrated analysis of multimodal single-cell data”, Cell, 2021 (Seurat v4 / Azimuth). Domínguez Conde et al., “Cross-tissue immune cell analysis reveals tissue-specific features in humans”, Science, 2022 (CellTypist). Szabo et al., “Single-cell transcriptomics of human T cells reveals tissue and activation signatures”, Nature Communications, 2019 for T cell state definitions.

Week 5. Borcherding et al., “scRepertoire”, F1000Research, 2020. Sturm et al., “Scirpy”, Bioinformatics, 2020. Dash et al., “Quantifiable predictive features define epitope-specific T cell receptor repertoires”, Nature, 2017 (TCRdist). Huang et al., “GLIPH2”, Nature Biotechnology, 2020. Zhang et al., “Lineage tracking reveals dynamic relationships of T cells in colorectal cancer”, Nature, 2018 (STARTRAC). Goncharov et al., “VDJdb in the pandemic era”, Nature Methods, 2022.

Week 6. Korsunsky et al., “Harmony”, Nature Methods, 2019. Luecken et al., “Benchmarking atlas-level data integration in single-cell genomics”, Nature Methods, 2022. Squair et al., “Confronting false discoveries in single-cell differential expression”, Nature Communications, 2021 (the pseudobulk argument). Zimmerman, Espeland & Langefeld, “A practical solution to pseudoreplication bias in single-cell studies”, Nature Communications, 2021. Phipson et al., “propeller”, Bioinformatics, 2022. Büttner et al., “scCODA”, Nature Communications, 2021.
